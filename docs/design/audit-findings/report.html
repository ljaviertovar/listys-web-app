What the vision applies, and observations

Applied in the “After” phones

  • DS-024 / DS-025: V4·19 empty state is reached and drawn as the info callout plus a full-width primary button.
  • DS-026: footer buttons 44px; “Change Usual Store” secondary.
  • DS-027 / DS-028: one grouped panel with dividers; 14px violet store name; tags inline; “Last … · N times”.

  • DS-030: white tiles with icon, 24px value, small unit, 12px padding. DS-031: category caption. DS-033: price-tag and repeat glyphs. DS-032: one-store callout.

Assumptions: V4·18 and V4·19 were not redrawn, so they take the v2 look; light theme only (the dark theme of the app is not reviewed, DS-010); icons are simplified, not the Hugeicons glyphs; the item name is hidden as in v2 unless the switch is on.

Design observations (advisory)

Non-blocking, no DS-IDs, not counted, not in the plan.

  • Hierarchy. The tiles (about 76px) sit above the comparison. With four or more stores the prices fall below the fold on a phone although the sheet is called Prices by Store; the list first and the history after is an option.
  • A smarter footer action. The decision this sheet supports is “should I change my usual store?”. When another store is cheaper, label the button with it (“Make Costco the usual store”) instead of the generic “Change Usual Store”.
  • Two tags on a long name. “Costco ◇ Cheapest ⇄ Usual” fits at 390px; with a 30-character store name the tags wrap under the name, so allow two lines in the row height.
  • Upload icon. The reference button has no icon; the dialog trigger adds one. Keeping it helps recognition; low priority.
  • Meaning of the tag glyphs. A price tag for “Cheapest” and loop arrows for “Usual” are not self-explanatory; the text carries the meaning, which is right, and they must stay text-labelled.

Withdrawn: DS-022 (Trips icon)

The first audit asked for the shopping bag on the Trips tab, following the DS rule “the cart means start shopping”. Your references V4·15 and V4·16 draw the cart there, as the code does, so the DS rule is what changes (decision 22); DS-022 no longer appears in the issue list.

Design observations (advisory)

Non-blocking, no DS-IDs, not counted, not in the plan.

  • The references disagree with themselves in two places: the chevron of an open section (up in V4·11, V4·13 and V4·16, down in V4·15) and the date line (time in V4·16, none in V4·15). Both are open decisions; redraw one of them so the next audit has a single target.
  • Six sections in one sheet. With every case present the review is about 2000px tall at phone width; the three “not on the receipt” sections carry no action. Keeping “Not checked, not on the receipt” folded (as built) is right; consider folding “Checked, not on the receipt” too.
  • Tiles are counts, not controls. They sit next to the “Change” links, so keep them without a hover or pointer cursor and with the hairline ring, so they are not read as buttons.
  • Receipt tile. The 56px tile is a good anchor for a real thumbnail later; until then a glyph repeated on every receipt row adds little, so keep the static version small and quiet (decision 21).
