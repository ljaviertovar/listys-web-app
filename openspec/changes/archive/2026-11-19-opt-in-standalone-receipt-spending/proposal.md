## Why

Some receipts are uploaded outside a trip but still represent spending the user wants to track.

## What Changes

- Let users opt individual standalone receipts into monthly and all-time Spending.
- Show the saved spending state in the receipt detail and an In spending indicator in the list while the receipt contributes.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `dashboard-overview`: standalone receipt opt-in.

## Impact

Receipt storage and API, receipt details and list, Spending aggregation.
